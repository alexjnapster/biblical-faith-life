# Independent Quality Assurance

Review the finished artifact against the full original request, raw passages, sources, and doctrinal units. Audit requirements, depth, argument, genre, language, length, evidence, and pastoral usefulness. Prioritize consequential defects, repair them substantively, then review the changed artifact again. Record reviewer identity, artifact hash, findings and dispositions; the reviewer must differ from the author. Do not self-certify perfection. Deterministic checks never stand in for an independent semantic review.
